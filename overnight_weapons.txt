asserted `hp < maxHp` after a blow thrown 0.32s after a successful parry. Commit

measuring the old behaviour. Fixed by ticking out the grace window before the
second swing, which is what the test actually meant to do. The relic behaviour
itself is unchanged and still asserted.
Committed separately from the feature so the PR diff does not look like it broke it.
