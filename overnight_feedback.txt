## One correction: the mitigation floor threshold is 100, not 58
You wrote: "with k=25 a defense of 58 is already at that floor."
mitigate() is attack * max(0.2, 1 - defense/(defense+k)).
At defense 58, k=25: 1 - 58/83 = 0.30, so the player still takes ~30% —
NOT at the 20% floor. The floor is reached at defense 100
(100/125 = 0.8). Your conclusion (late armour trivialises enemy damage)
still stands, but use 100 as the threshold number when you tune,
or you will overshoot the fix.

## One context note for the log (not an error)
Your TTK section evaluates each enemy at its minDepth, at full stamina

numbers are player-optimistic. Fine for before/after comparison, but say
so next to the baseline so nobody reads them as real-session values.
